**Решение:** port

- `DashboardSidebarToggle`: `aria-expanded = sidebarOpen`; `DashboardSidebarCollapse`: `aria-expanded = !sidebarCollapsed`; `Header`: `:aria-expanded="open"` у кнопки меню.
